Current Projects

Our research integrates high-resolution mass spectrometry, ambient ionization, and public health surveillance to address urgent toxicological challenges in San Francisco and beyond.

PRJ-01 · PUBLIC HEALTH SURVEILLANCE ZSFG Ward 93 & SFDPH

Ward 93 Drug Surveillance with DPH

In close collaboration with the ZSFG Ward 93 Opioid Treatment Program and the San Francisco Department of Public Health (SFDPH), this longitudinal surveillance initiative monitors real-world shifts in the local illicit drug supply. Using comprehensive LC-HRMS urine toxicology testing, we track the emergence and co-exposure patterns of synthetic opioids (fentanyl analogs, nitazenes), veterinary sedative adulterants (xylazine, medetomidine), and novel designer benzodiazepines (bromazolam) to inform clinical addiction care and municipal overdose prevention strategies.

Key Analytes Fentanyl Analogs, Xylazine, Bromazolam, Medetomidine
Partners ZSFG Ward 93 OTP & SF Dept. of Public Health
Project Team Kara Lynch, Lauren Bishop, Judah Iwawaki
PRJ-02 · CLINICAL LC-HRMS SCIEX X500R QTOF

Comprehensive Drug Testing in the ZSFG Clinical Lab via X500R

Conventional targeted panels and immunoassays frequently miss unscheduled or unexpected toxic exposures in emergency department and ICU patients. We deploy the SCIEX X500R Quadrupole Time-of-Flight (QTOF) high-resolution mass spectrometer in the ZSFG Core Clinical Laboratory to perform broad-spectrum screening across 239 drugs, illicit substances, and metabolites. Our work systematically optimizes Information-Dependent Acquisition (IDA) and Sequential Window Acquisition of All Theoretical Mass Spectra (SWATH-MS) workflows for rapid clinical turnaround.

Instrumentation SCIEX X500R QTOF LC-HRMS (SWATH / IDA)
Spectral Coverage 239 Drugs
Project Team Kara Lynch, Gillian Rexroad, Priscilla Yeung, Michael Metrick
PRJ-03 · AMBIENT IONIZATION DART-HRMS / DART-MS/MS

Investigating DART Mass Spectrometry

Direct Analysis in Real Time (DART) mass spectrometry enables ambient ionization directly from solid, liquid, or swabbed surfaces without chromatographic separation. We are investigating DART-MS coupled to high-resolution and tandem mass spectrometers for sub-minute screening of drug checking samples, adulterated pharmaceuticals, biological fluids, and rapid clinical toxicology triage where time-to-result is critical.

Ionization Mode Ambient Metastable Helium Desorption Ionization
Analysis Speed < 30 Seconds per Specimen (Chromatography-Free)
Project Team Kara Lynch, Priscilla Yeung, Gillian Rexroad, Lauren Bishop
PRJ-04 · BOTANICAL TOXICOLOGY Mitragyna speciosa

Investigating Kratom Alkaloids

Commercial products derived from Mitragyna speciosa (Kratom) contain complex mixtures of indole and oxindole alkaloids with atypical opioid and adrenergic pharmacology, including semi-synthetic concentrated 7-hydroxymitragynine preparations. Because mitragynine shares an exact mass with diastereomers such as speciociliatine, speciogynine, and mitraciliatine, we are developing high-resolution chromatographic and mass spectrometric methods to resolve and quantify individual Kratom alkaloids and active metabolites in clinical specimens.

Target Alkaloids Mitragynine, 7-OH-Mitragynine, Speciociliatine, Paynantheine
Analytical Challenge Isobaric Diastereomer Resolution & Metabolite Profiling
Project Team Kara Lynch, Hannah Lusk, Lauren Bishop
PRJ-05 · QUANTITATIVE BIOMARKERS LC-MS/MS Quantitation

THC / Nicotine Quantitation

Accurate low-picogram/nanogram quantitation of cannabinoids and tobacco alkaloids is essential for clinical toxicology, pediatric accidental ingestion evaluation, maternal-fetal exposure studies, and public health epidemiology. Our laboratory develops and validates robust isotope-dilution LC-MS/MS assays for Δ9-THC, 11-OH-THC, THC-COOH, cannabidiol (CBD), nicotine, cotinine, trans-3'-hydroxycotinine, and anabasine across serum, plasma, urine, and alternative matrices.

Cannabinoid Panel Δ9-THC, 11-OH-THC, THC-COOH, CBD, CBN
Nicotine Panel Nicotine, Cotinine, 3-OH-Cotinine, Anabasine
Project Team Kara Lynch, Emilio Mejia
