Emeritus Members

Former fellows, residents, and postdoctoral scholars from the Lynch Laboratory who now lead clinical chemistry, toxicology, and diagnostic programs across academic medicine and industry.

XW

Xander van Wijk, PhD, DABCC, FADLM

Former Clinical Chemistry & Toxicology Fellow
Current Position
Senior Director of Medical and Scientific Affairs, Beckman Coulter Diagnostics; Associate Clinical Professor (Volunteer), Department of Laboratory Medicine, UCSF (formerly Assistant Professor of Pathology & Director of Clinical Chemistry, University of Chicago).
Projects with Dr. Kara Lynch
Trained as a Clinical Chemistry Postdoctoral Fellow at UCSF/ZSFG, where he collaborated with Dr. Lynch on high-resolution mass spectrometry (LC-HRMS) comprehensive drug screening, identification of novel psychoactive substances (synthetic opioids and designer benzodiazepines), toxicokinetic profiling in acute overdose cases, and clinical toxicology immunoassay interference investigations.

MM

Morgan W. Mann, PhD, DABCC

Former Joint Clinical Chemistry Fellow (UCSF / Stanford)
Current Position
Assistant Professor (CHS), Division of Genetics and Metabolism, Department of Pediatrics, University of Wisconsin–Madison; Assistant Director, Newborn Screening Program, Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene (WSLH).
Projects with Dr. Kara Lynch
During his Joint UCSF/Stanford Clinical Chemistry Fellowship, Dr. Mann led analytical toxicology studies with Dr. Lynch comparing Information-Dependent Acquisition (IDA) and Sequential Window Acquisition of All Theoretical Mass Spectra (SWATH-MS) on QTOF instrumentation, as well as investigating electron-activated dissociation (EAD) to improve untargeted clinical drug screening sensitivity and specificity.

CO

Cody Orahoske, PhD, NRCC, C(ASCP)

Former Clinical Chemistry Postdoctoral Fellow
Current Position
Clinical Chemist & Technical Director, Labcorp.
Projects with Dr. Kara Lynch
Completed his Clinical Chemistry Fellowship at UCSF and ZSFG under Dr. Lynch's mentorship, where he developed and validated targeted LC-MS/MS and high-resolution mass spectrometry assays to monitor emerging illicit drug adulterants on the West Coast—most notably characterizing the early rise of xylazine and its metabolites in San Francisco patient populations.

JW

Jeffrey D. Whitman, MD, MS

Former Laboratory Medicine Resident & Collaborator
Current Position
Associate Professor of Laboratory Medicine, University of California, San Francisco (UCSF); Director of Clinical Microbiology, Zuckerberg San Francisco General Hospital (ZSFG).
Projects with Dr. Kara Lynch
Collaborated extensively with Dr. Lynch during his residency and faculty tenure at ZSFG on high-resolution mass spectrometry acquisition strategies (evaluating SWATH-MS versus IDA workflows for clinical toxicology drug screening), translational LC-HRMS metabolomics for infectious disease diagnostics, and large-scale clinical evaluation of diagnostic assays at ZSFG.

RL

Ruben Yiqi Luo, PhD, DABCC, FADLM

Former Clinical Chemistry Fellow
Current Position
Assistant Professor of Pathology, Stanford University School of Medicine; Associate Director, Clinical Chemistry Laboratory, Stanford Health Care; Co-Director, Stanford–UCSF Joint Clinical Chemistry Fellowship.
Projects with Dr. Kara Lynch
Trained as a Clinical Chemistry Fellow with Dr. Lynch at UCSF/ZSFG, where he spearheaded the creation of a High-Resolution LC-MS/MS Spectral Library for screening toxic natural products (botanical, fungal, and marine toxins), advanced untargeted QTOF data-independent acquisition methods, and continues to co-direct joint UCSF–Stanford fellowship training and mass spectrometry research with Dr. Lynch.
